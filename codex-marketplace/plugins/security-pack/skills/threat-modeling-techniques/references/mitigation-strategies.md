# Mitigation Strategies

Use the smallest effective control that reduces the risk to an acceptable
level.

## Four Approaches

1. Eliminate the attack surface when the feature is not necessary.
2. Reduce the likelihood or impact with controls and constraints.
3. Transfer the risk to a third party or external service when justified.
4. Accept the risk only with explicit approval, monitoring, and review.

## Control Types

- Preventive controls stop threats before they occur.
- Detective controls identify threats in progress or after occurrence.
- Corrective controls restore the system after detection.

## Defense in Depth

Layer multiple controls so that the failure of one control does not expose the
system by itself.
