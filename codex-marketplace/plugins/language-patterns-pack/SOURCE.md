## Boundary

Only the retained TypeScript, Python, and other language/framework testing and pattern guidance is kept here. The pack does not absorb frontend, architecture, CQRS, database, security, repo governance, CI, or other non-language guidance.

The `python-testing-patterns` retained source snapshot also contains `validation/rubric.yaml`; that rubric stays in retained source custody and is not projected into the installable pack.

ECC skills are projected verbatim from retained ECC source custody under `sources/third_party/ecc/upstream/skills/` with MIT license attribution to upstream author `affaan-m/ECC`.
