# AI Agency

Use this skill to break agency work into roles, checkpoints, and explicit
hand-offs.

## What it preserves

- orchestration over improvisation
- clear responsibility splits
- review and routing checkpoints
- agentic workflow sequencing

## When to use

- The work spans multiple roles or specialists.
- You need planning, execution, and review to stay separate.
- You want a market asset for agency-style operations.
