# Design

Use this skill to keep design work grounded in reviewable decisions.

## What it preserves

- design critique patterns
- hierarchy and clarity checks
- reusable review prompts
- small, inspectable guidance

## When to use

- You need to evaluate a UI, flow, or brand direction.
- You want design advice as an operational skill, not a paragraph of opinion.

## Source anchors

- Upstream family root: `plugins/design`

## Market output

This skill turns the upstream design family into a plugin skill with a clear
market-facing purpose.
