# Zoom-out failure modes

Use these failure modes to classify why a compressed model is not green.

## Flattening

The model erases distinction, assigns shared joke forms or interaction patterns too exclusively, or makes one entity
carry a behaviour that belongs to several people or to a room.

## Overresolution

The model resolves motive, awareness, intent, identity, or ambiguity beyond what artifacts support. Do not turn
unconscious or unstable behaviour into conscious self-knowledge unless source surfaces support it.

## Externalising

The model treats room members, participants, or internal actors as outside observers when artifacts show they are inside
the interaction.

## Symbolic drift

The model becomes `X represents Y` instead of a behavioural model that can regenerate actual artifact shape. Symbol can
be useful, but it cannot replace behaviour.

## Regeneration failure

The model cannot plausibly reproduce timing, voice, interaction pattern, artifact sequence, or failure mode. This is a
hard red for persistence.

## Artifact gap

The model may be promising but has not been tested against the relevant artifacts. This is amber, not green.

## False green patterns

- The model explains one artifact but damages a broader pattern.
- The model sharpens a theme while weakening voice.
- The model makes a character easier to summarize but less distinct.
- The model resolves a contradiction that should remain productive ambiguity.
- The model is conversation-useful but is presented as repo-grounded truth.
