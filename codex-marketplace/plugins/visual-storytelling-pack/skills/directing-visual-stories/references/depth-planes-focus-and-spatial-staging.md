# Depth planes, focus, and spatial staging

Foreground, middle-ground, and background are simultaneous story layers. Assign
each a job before treating depth as atmosphere:

- **Foreground:** entry, obstruction, witness position, tactile evidence, scale,
  or a boundary the audience must cross.
- **Middle-ground:** the active beat, exchange, route, or causal action.
- **Background:** place, history, pressure, consequence, foreshadowing, or a
  second action that changes the meaning of the middle-ground.

These layers may be literal photographic planes, a large matte behind inset panels,
stacked graphic fields, or tile/world layers. They need not all be equally detailed.

## Focus strategies

- **Shallow focus:** isolates a subject, restricts knowledge, or makes a detail
  intimate. Keep the blur meaningful; it can conceal a person or cause.
- **Deep focus:** makes relationships across planes simultaneously available and
  gives the audience discovery agency. Strengthen scale and vectors so hierarchy
  does not collapse.
- **Rack focus:** transfers attention or changes the interpretation of an already
  visible relationship. State the before and after subjects.
- **Split-field focus:** binds separated planes when simultaneous cause/effect,
  observer/observed, or two-sided knowledge is the beat.
- **Graphic or flattened depth:** suppresses natural perspective to make pattern,
  emblem, social arrangement, or readable navigation primary.

Focus is a promise about knowledge, not a prestige effect. If an important causal
relationship is hidden by shallow focus, either make the concealment intentional
or choose deep staging.

## Occlusion and causality

Layer bodies, architecture, foliage, light, reflections, or interface elements to
show what the audience can and cannot know. Occlusion can create surveillance,
confinement, proximity, or an approaching threat. Preserve enough evidence that the
audience can infer the relation you intend. Ask whether the background changes the
interpretation of the front action, rather than adding “depth” as decoration.

## Direction format

For each plane state: subject/evidence, depth relation, detail level, focus state,
light/palette relationship, and whether it is allowed to move or change. State the
causal read: “foreground bars establish the witness position; the mid-ground hand
passes the key; the background door opens late, making the hand an escape attempt.”

Review both full-frame and reduced-size versions. The planes should collapse into a
clear hierarchy at a thumbnail while retaining optional discoveries at full size.
