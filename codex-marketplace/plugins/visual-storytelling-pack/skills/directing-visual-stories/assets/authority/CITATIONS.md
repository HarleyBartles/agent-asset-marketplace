# Authority record for directing-visual-stories

## Scholarly citation

The operational material is a first-party synthesis. The following canonical
sources informed the research spike and are cited for their concepts, not copied
as operational prose. Retrieved 2026-09-15.

### Photography and framing

- Adobe. “The basics of photography composition.” https://www.adobe.com/uk/creativecloud/photography/discover/photo-composition.html — compositional tools such as balance, rule of thirds, and visual weight.
- Adobe. “A guide to leading lines in photography.” https://www.adobe.com/creativecloud/photography/technique/leading-lines.html — directional vectors that guide attention.
- Adobe. “What is framing in photography?” https://www.adobe.com/creativecloud/photography/technique/framing.html — using elements within the scene to frame a subject.
- Adobe. “Fill the frame photography.” https://www.adobe.com/creativecloud/photography/technique/fill-the-frame.html — exclusion and proximity as emphasis.
- Nikon. “5 Easy Composition Guidelines.” https://www.nikonusa.com/learn-and-explore/c/tips-and-techniques/5-easy-composition-guidelines — practical framing, balance, and perspective guidance.
- Kodak. “Keep it All in Perspective—Aspect Ratio Targets.” https://www.kodak.com/en/motion/page/aspect-ratio-targets/ — aspect ratio as a compositional and delivery constraint.

### Cinematography and visual storytelling

- American Film Institute. “What is Cinematography?” https://www.afi.com/news/what-is-cinematography-and-what-does-a-cinematographer-do/ — camera, light, and image choices serving story.
- American Society of Cinematographers. “Shot Craft: Where Do You Put the Camera?” https://theasc.com/article/shot-craft-where-do-you-put-the-camera/ — viewpoint, coverage, and audience relationship.
- American Society of Cinematographers. “Realism for Citizen Kane.” https://theasc.com/article/realism-for-citizen-kane/ — deep staging and focus as narrative tools.
- American Society of Cinematographers. “Photographing The Andromeda Strain.” https://theasc.com/articles/the-andromeda-strain-may-1971 — camera placement, spatial tension, and visual continuity.
- American Society of Cinematographers. “Sunrise: A Song of Two Humans—Lauding a Landmark.” https://theasc.com/article/sunrise-almendros/ — expressive camera movement and atmosphere in service of feeling.
- American Society of Cinematographers. “Terror Through Lighting.” https://theasc.com/article/terror-through-lighting/ — selective visibility and lighting as emotional narration.

### Comics, editorial, and theatre

- Library of Congress. “Comic Book and Graphic Narrative.” https://www.loc.gov/exhibits/timely-and-timeless/comic-book-and-graphic-narrative.html — panel, page, sequence, and reader-directed narrative relationships.
- Library of Congress. “The Narratology of Comic Art.” https://www.loc.gov/item/2020718224/ — comics as spatial and temporal storytelling.
- Library of Congress. “Image Description Best Practices.” https://www.loc.gov/nls/services-and-resources/studio-resources/image-description-best-practices/ — describing visual evidence and relationships without relying on sight alone.
- AIGA Design Teaching Resource. “Multi-Page Layout Magazine Design Project.” https://teachingresource.aiga.org/project/magazine-design-project/ — page systems, spreads, hierarchy, and editorial pacing.
- AIGA Eye on Design. “Type as Image and Lettering as Message.” https://eyeondesign.aiga.org/making-rules-breaking-rules-the-art-of-magazine-typography/ — type as a spatial and expressive actor.
- AIGA Eye on Design. “Design for Sale.” https://eyeondesign.aiga.org/design-for-sale-a-template-for-magazine-design-16-years-in-the-remaking/ — template, variation, and editorial identity across a publication.
- Smashing Magazine. “Inspired Design Decisions: Pressing Matters.” https://www.smashingmagazine.com/2019/07/inspired-design-decisions-pressing-matters/ — editorial rhythm, hierarchy, and material voice.
- Smashing Magazine. “When Editors Design.” https://www.smashingmagazine.com/2013/06/controlling-presentation-in-structured-content/ — editorial control over structured content and presentation.
- ETC. “A Guide for Lighting the Stage.” https://www.etcconnect.com/workarea/DownloadAsset.aspx?id=10737493970 — stage visibility, focus, mood, and composition through light.
- ETC. “Objectives of Lighting Design.” https://blog.etcconnect.com/stage-lighting-design-part-2-objectives-of-lighting-design — visibility, composition, mood, and temporal support as lighting objectives.
- Theatrecrafts. “What is Lighting Design.” https://theatrecrafts.com/pages/home/topics/lighting/what-is-lighting-design/ — lighting as audience guidance and temporal design.
- Theatrecrafts. “Lighting Design—The Process.” https://theatrecrafts.com/pages/home/topics/lighting/lighting-design-process/ — translating a production concept into cues and a lighting plan.
- Open Textbook Library. “An Introduction to Technical Theatre.” https://open.umn.edu/opentextbooks/textbooks/650 — staging, blocking, audience relationship, and production vocabulary.

### Anime, games, and constrained visual systems

- Studio Ghibli. “Studio Ghibli Layout Exhibition.” https://www.ghibli.jp/info/004840/ — layout as a complete shot blueprint integrating environment, pose, and movement.
- Bandai Namco Pictures. “How Anime is Made.” https://www.bn-pictures.co.jp/business/making.html — production roles and layout-to-animation translation.
- Toei Animation. “Animation Production.” https://corp.toei-anim.co.jp/en/company/animation_production.html — staged animation workflow and timing decisions.
- SIGGRAPH Asia. “R&D for Anime Production.” https://research.archinc.jp/static/files/siggraphasia2021-anime-rnd.pdf — layout, compositing, and production constraints.
- GDC Vault. “A Narrative Approach to Level Design.” https://www.gdcvault.com/play/1024302/Level-Design-Workshop-A-Narrative — spatial composition serving authored narrative beats.
- GDC Vault. “What Happened Here? Environmental Storytelling.” https://www.gdcvault.com/play/1012647/what-happened-here-environmental — environmental evidence and player inference.
- Game Developer. “Composition in Level Design.” https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/composition-in-level-design — landmarks, sightlines, spatial rhythm, and player attention.
- Game Developer. “Level Design Patterns in 2D Games.” https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/level-design-patterns-in-2d-games — reusable spatial patterns and readable 2D navigation.
- Ubisoft. “Level Design.” https://www.ubisoft.com/en-us/company/careers/our-jobs/design-creative-direction/level-design — player experience, pacing, and navigable space as authored design concerns.
- Commodore. “Amiga Hardware Reference Manual—Bitplanes and Color.” https://amigadev.elowar.com/read/ADCD_2.1/Hardware_Manual_guide/node0066.html — display layers, palette roles, and constrained visual separation.
- Commodore. “Amiga Hardware Reference Manual—The Color Table.” https://amigadev.elowar.com/read/ADCD_2.1/Hardware_Manual_guide/node0067.html — palette indexing and display colour constraints.
- Commodore. “Amiga Hardware Reference Manual—About the Copper.” https://amigadev.elowar.com/read/ADCD_2.1/Hardware_Manual_guide/node0048.html — timed display changes as a compositional and atmospheric technique.
- Electronic Arts. “DeluxePaint II Manual.” https://d1yx3ys82bpsa0.cloudfront.net/atchm/documents/DeluxePaint_II_manual.pdf — constrained raster tools and deliberate pixel/palette construction.
- Mark Ferrari. “8 Bit & ‘8 Bitish’ Graphics—Outside the Box.” https://www.gdcvault.com/play/1023586/8-Bit-8-Bitish-Graphics — palette tricks, animation, and expressive constraint-led graphics.
