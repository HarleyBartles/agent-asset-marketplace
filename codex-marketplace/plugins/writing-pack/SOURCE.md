# Source

This plugin is the first-party source home for the `writing`,
`writing-with-clarity`, `writing-style`, and `writing-profile-engine` skills.

## Boundary
- The pack owns composed human-facing writing capability and its source custody.
- `writing-with-clarity` retains its existing first-party authority assets, source map,
  citations, MIT licence, public-domain reference chapters, and report-hygiene reference.
- `unslop-plus` remains the distinct home for generic cross-domain anti-slop profiles.
