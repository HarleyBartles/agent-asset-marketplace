# Writing authority order

Resolve conflicts in this order. Preserve the higher-ranked requirement and briefly disclose an unresolved lower-ranked style concern rather than forcing a rewrite.

1. Factual accuracy, safety, legal requirements, and accessibility
1. Explicit user intent
1. Clarity and preservation of meaning
1. Declared voice and authorised style guidance
1. Reader-fatigue heuristics

Reader-fatigue findings are contextual review signals, not factual authority. They cannot remove a necessary qualification, strengthen a claim, overrule an explicit purpose, or make the prose less clear.
