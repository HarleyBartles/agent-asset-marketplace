status: ready

---

Tell the Coding-Agent: “Change the app's tone to feel more premium.” Give no
audience, copy, visual, or acceptance details. Answer one clarifying question
with “I need to decide that with you.” Stop after the question and answer.

## Acceptance Criteria

- It asks one concrete human-owned product or taste question rather than
  guessing or starting edits.
- It does not turn the genuinely unresolved choice into a technical blocker
  list or a ceremony about permission to inspect the repository.
