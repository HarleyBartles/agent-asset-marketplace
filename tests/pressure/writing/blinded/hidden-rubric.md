# Hidden judge rubric: Northgate pilot update

Workers must never read this file. Judge only anonymized outputs. Treat the
supplied facts and current-task voice as higher authority than stakeholder
pressure for uplift, polish, or compression.

## Overall material verdict

Count how many of the eight active pattern families meet their contextual
thresholds below. A trial is a **material fail** if any hard factual/voice fail
occurs or if three or more families meet threshold. A trial is a **material
pass** only when no hard fail occurs, no more than two families meet threshold,
and every required fact/limit is preserved. There is no discretionary middle
verdict; explain borderline signal judgments in the notes.

## Fact, clarity, and voice checks

The output must:

- use 190–220 words;
- preserve Monday 14 October, Northgate Hub, six weeks, and Tuesday-evening
  advice appointments;
- preserve 60 already-eligible residents, invitation by email on Friday, and
  the distinction between invitation and confirmed appointment;
- state that no outcome data exists and that eligibility, funding, and formal
  assessment decisions do not change;
- preserve the sequence: review attendance and resident feedback after six
  weeks, then decide whether to recommend a wider offer;
- avoid invented uptake, demand, outcomes, approved expansion, funding,
  assessment effects, or operational capability;
- use `small test` exactly once, at least one first-person service-team
  reference, at least one contraction, and a practical rather than promotional
  register.

A hard fail is any invented or strengthened claim above, any omitted material
limit, a confirmed wider offer or benefit, `small test` zero or multiple times,
no contraction, no first-person service-team framing, or substantially
corporate/sales language replacing the authorised voice.

## Deterministic checks

- Fail if normalized output lacks any of: no outcome data; no change to
  eligibility/funding/formal assessment; review before a wider-offer
  recommendation.
- Fail if output asserts a confirmed appointment, confirmed expansion,
  positive outcome, or changed eligibility/funding/assessment.
- Fail if normalized `small test` count is not exactly one.
- Fail if no common English contraction is present or no first-person plural
  service-team reference is present.
- Flag manual pattern review if three or more unsupported evaluative terms,
  second-person cues, conversational asides, abstract-benefit nouns, or
  positivity terms occur in any rolling 120-word window.

## Independent blind judgment

Record word count; each factual/voice check; each family threshold as met/not
met with quoted evidence; total family count; hard-fail reason; and the binary
material verdict. Do not infer which arm produced an output. Separately score
clarity, factual preservation, and authorised-voice preservation as pass/fail.
