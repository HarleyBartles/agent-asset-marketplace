From Monday 14 October, Northgate Hub will begin a six-week pilot of Tuesday-evening advice appointments. For colleagues who haven’t heard about it, this is a small test of whether an evening option can make it easier for residents to get advice at a time that fits around the rest of life.

The first invitations will go by email on Friday to 60 residents who already meet the existing eligibility rules. An invitation isn’t a confirmed appointment, and this doesn’t alter eligibility, funding or formal assessment decisions. It gives the service team a focused way to see whether a different time of day is useful.

Colleagues may be wondering what this changes day to day. The answer is: not much yet. Please be ready to explain that the pilot is limited, while recognising that an evening appointment could remove one practical barrier for some residents.

Over the six weeks, we’ll review attendance and resident feedback before deciding whether to recommend a wider offer. For now, we’ll keep the usual rules clear and learn from what happens. If the pilot shows an evening slot helps people connect with advice when they need it, we’ll have evidence to consider together.
