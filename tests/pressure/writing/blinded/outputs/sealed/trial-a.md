From Monday 14 October, Northgate Hub will run a six-week pilot of Tuesday-evening advice appointments. For colleagues who have not heard about the work, it comes from a straightforward question: can we make it easier for residents to speak with us at a time that fits around work, caring and the rest of life? We are calling it a small test, but it is a useful chance to learn what helps.

Sixty residents who already meet the existing eligibility rules will receive email invitations on Friday. An invitation is not a confirmed appointment, and nothing about eligibility, funding or formal assessment decisions is changing. The point is to offer another route into the same service, with a little more room for people whose days do not follow office hours.

For us, this means listening carefully as well as running the appointments. The service team will review attendance and resident feedback after six weeks. We will then decide whether there is a sound basis to recommend a wider offer. There is no result to announce yet; the work is to find out, properly, whether the evening approach makes a worthwhile difference.

Thank you to everyone helping get this under way. Whatever we find, we will have learned directly from residents and moved the service forward with care.
