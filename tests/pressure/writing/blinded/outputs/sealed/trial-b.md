Northgate Hub is trying a simple change from Monday 14 October: for six weeks, we’ll offer Tuesday-evening advice appointments. For colleagues who haven’t followed the work, this is a small test of whether a different time can make it easier for residents to get advice when daytime slots are awkward.

On Friday, we’ll email invitations to 60 residents who already meet the existing eligibility rules. An invitation isn’t an appointment, so nobody needs to read more into it than that. Eligibility, funding and formal assessment decisions stay exactly as they are.

What might this mean for us? It’s a chance to see whether a more flexible point of contact works better for residents and helps the service stay connected to the realities of their week. We don’t have results yet, and we shouldn’t pretend otherwise. The useful evidence will come from attendance and from what residents tell us.

After six weeks, the service team will review both before deciding whether to recommend a wider offer. For now, the job is straightforward: make the welcome clear, handle questions with the usual care, and help us learn. If this proves useful, it will be because colleagues made a modest change work well for residents.
