From Monday 14 October, Northgate Hub will begin a six-week pilot of Tuesday-evening advice appointments. It’s a small test of whether a later slot makes it easier for residents who already use the service to get the advice they need.

On Friday, 60 residents who meet the existing eligibility rules will receive an email invitation. An invitation isn’t a confirmed appointment, and the pilot doesn’t change eligibility, funding or formal assessment decisions. Those parts stay as they are.

For colleagues, this is a chance to learn whether a different time of day helps residents connect with us more easily. The change is modest, but the question behind it matters: can we offer support at a time that fits more comfortably around people’s lives? We’ll need the usual care with invitations and appointments, alongside a willingness to notice what residents tell us.

After six weeks, the service team will review attendance and resident feedback before deciding whether to recommend a wider offer. We don’t have outcome data yet, so there’s nothing to declare early. But we do have a useful opportunity to listen, learn and improve the experience for residents, where it counts.
