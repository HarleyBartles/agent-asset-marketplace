From Monday 14 October, Northgate Hub will begin a six-week pilot of Tuesday-evening advice appointments. This is a small test of whether an evening option can make it easier for some residents to speak with us around the rest of their week. It doesn’t replace the service we already provide, and it isn’t a change to who can use it.

On Friday, we’ll email invitations to 60 residents who already meet the existing eligibility rules. An invitation isn’t a confirmed appointment; it simply lets someone know the option is available. If you work with residents, please keep that distinction clear. The pilot doesn’t change eligibility, funding or formal assessment decisions, so the usual routes and safeguards remain in place.

For colleagues at the Hub, this gives us a chance to learn how an evening session works in practice and what residents value. There will be new questions and a few ordinary wrinkles; that’s what a pilot is for. We should be open about what we learn, rather than assuming that a later slot is automatically better.

After six weeks, the service team will review attendance and resident feedback before deciding whether to recommend a wider offer. For now, let’s make the start straightforward, helpful and properly attentive. If it improves access, we’ll have evidence to take the next step with confidence.
