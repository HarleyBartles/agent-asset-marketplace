Over recent months, colleagues at Northgate Hub have been looking at how advice can fit more comfortably around residents’ lives. Some people cannot easily attend during the day, so from Monday 14 October we’ll begin a six-week pilot of Tuesday-evening advice appointments.

This is a small test of whether an additional evening option can make it simpler for people to get advice when they need it. On Friday, sixty residents who already meet the existing eligibility rules will receive an email invitation. That invitation is not a confirmed appointment, and nothing about eligibility, funding or formal assessment decisions is changing.

For colleagues, this should mean a clearer way to support residents whose schedules do not line up with daytime appointments. It is not a promise of a wider service yet; there is no outcome data at this stage. We’ll use the six weeks to learn from attendance and resident feedback, rather than make assumptions from a spreadsheet.

At the end of the pilot, the service team will review what we’ve learned before deciding whether to recommend any wider offer. Please help residents feel welcome if they’re invited, and keep sharing what you notice. This is a practical chance to make the service more reachable, and we’ll take it seriously.
