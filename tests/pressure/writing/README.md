# Writing pressure campaign

This campaign supplies reusable instruction-following and boundary scenarios.
It does not assess authorship, universal prose quality, or detector accuracy.

`prompts/` contains the direct pressure scenarios. `blinded/` contains the
frozen A/B stimulus, judge-only rubric, campaign contract, and evaluator
configuration. Generated responses, judgments, scores, hashes, and run
chronology are transient and are not committed.

Each fixture is synthetic or records its provenance limitation. No pressure
artifact contains a private author corpus.

The scenarios do not authorize detector evasion, authorship inference, or
intentional degradation of prose.
