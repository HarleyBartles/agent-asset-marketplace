# AGENTS.md

Scope: `.agents/playbooks/`

Playbooks are topical workflows available to an agent whenever their concern applies. They bind capability skills, doctrine, contracts, local commands, and evidence for one concern.

`Runbook routing` optionally records lifecycle runbooks that commonly route to a playbook; a playbook remains valid and directly available without such a link. Playbooks may compose or hand off to other playbooks, but they do not take ownership of lifecycle stages or become substitute homes for durable doctrine.
