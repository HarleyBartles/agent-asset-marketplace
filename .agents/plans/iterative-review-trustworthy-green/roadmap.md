## Epic invariants

- No plan may weaken the green predicate in the source spec.
- Each plan uses test-driven development and lands as a reviewable draft pull request before the next plan is written.
- Version-1 state, reports, and metrics may inform migration diagnostics but may not satisfy a version-2 green gate.
- A known `accepted-risk` finding may produce `reviewed-with-exceptions` but never green.
- A stored seal is only a candidate proof; human-facing green requires a fresh remote head, authority, and hosted-check revalidation.
- Review dispatches follow a monotonic scope-to-reasoning ladder: broader aperture or greater consequence raises the minimum capability and reasoning floor; a lower tier may discover findings but may never satisfy a higher-tier obligation.
- The blind final reviewer and closure auditor run in fresh independent contexts through an effective harness-defined custom profile such as `reviewer-strong` only when a trusted authority outside the reviewed head qualifies that exact semantic role. Baked model/reasoning is trusted and not overridden; wrong-role or anchored profiles, self-qualification, missing runtime receipts, unresolved routes, and silent downgrade block.
- Local state, check/report JSON, and hashes prove consistency but not execution provenance. Authority discovery, command execution, profile resolution, reviewer launch/completion, and remote observations require independently retrievable trusted-adapter receipts selected by a sealed composition-root trust policy; caller-selected issuers/locators, reused launch challenges, or unavailable receipt support block.
- Every mutation holds an exclusive state lock across load, ingestion, validation, history, and compare-and-swap replacement. Immutable content identity is separate from epoch-bound evidence identity, so concurrent findings cannot be lost and unchanged bytes can remain historically bound across replacement epochs.
- Cutover requires both deterministic state-soundness tests and 100% per-trial recall of the versioned frontier-reference issue set for every supported weaker-model/profile combination.
- A valid in-scope issue first found by an independent external frontier reviewer after internal green is a frontier escape: the benchmark pass is revoked, the missed tier is classified, and the case becomes a permanent regression fixture.
- Source edits remain under `codex-marketplace/plugins/superpowers-plus/skills/iterative-review/` and `codex-marketplace/plugins/superpowers-plus/skills/selecting-a-subagent/`; `.agents/skills/` remains generated.
- Consumer repositories supply local commands and domain obligations. Portable source must not assume this repository's paths outside explicit repo-local adapters.
- Until Plan 7 cuts over and passes the benchmark plus pressure campaign, the existing skill must be described as review assistance, not proof of reviewed green.
- Synthetic adapters may test kernel logic only after a pre-Plan-1 live capability gate proves that every production receipt/tool-confinement route exists outside candidate control. Missing custody or retrieval blocks implementation instead of being abstracted away.
- All JSON ingress rejects duplicate keys before canonicalization; receipt verification binds full review/dispatch/epoch/fingerprint scope, not only a subject digest.

## Plan acceptance boundaries

### Plan 1 - Evidence kernel

- A published pre-kernel capability matrix proves external issuer/locator custody, independent retrieval, one-time execution identity, exact raw-byte preservation, and blind-tool confinement for authority discovery, profile resolution, review launch/completion, command execution, ready transition, remote observation, and tool results. Failure stops before the synthetic kernel.
- Strict version-2 state, non-circular expected-authority/receipt projections, impact/challenged-coverage and canonical hypothesis records, typed consequence floors, structured exemption proof, sealed receipt/local-check/evidence-ingestion/hypothesis policies, duplicate-key rejection, review-owned immutable content plus epoch evidence bindings, cross-reference validation, chained history, locked compare-and-swap writes, discriminated feedback/review/check finding lifecycles, reserved ready-to-CI stage, monotonic re-ascent, and transient green evaluation exist behind one CLI. Plan 1 validates synthetic adapter outputs only after the capability gate and blocks live receipt-dependent actions without trusted adapters; Plans 2-6 supply live acquisition/execution adapters.
- Every known version-1 false-green/dead-route defect is a fixture proving legacy state cannot satisfy version 2; only the current baseline routing-test inconsistency is repaired.
- Missing one green predicate, incomplete authority manifest, unverified/fabricated receipt, skipped or downgraded tier, wrong-role/overridden final profile, unqualified fallback, unsafe evidence source, unsupported exemption, stale bytes, persisted green, accepted risk, and malformed remote observation all fail closed.

### Plan 2 - Snapshot and authority

- A sealed versioned discovery policy rooted in an external or base authority—not the reviewed head—defines repository/directory-law and PR roots, typed authority edges, recursive fixed-point traversal, canonical deduplication, and ambiguity behavior. Trusted connector discovery materializes full repository/PR/base/head/tree/diff/metadata identity, typed feedback, all sealed-policy identities/digests, and loaded/unavailable authority records into one canonical manifest payload. Its independently retrievable receipt binds the complete snapshot subject/fingerprint, discovery-policy source/version/digest, and typed manifest digest; omission and self-qualification fixtures cover every root/edge.
- A sealed provider-history policy enumerates all actionable threads/change requests, including items resolved before initial freeze. Each creates a feedback authority and immutable provider/thread-keyed finding unless already closed by this review lifecycle. Provider Resolve changes the unresolved subset but cannot erase the finding; fixed/false-positive proof remains required. Live omission fixtures include resolve-before-freeze and resolve-between-refresh cases.
- Base/head/tree, content-bearing PR scope/metadata, governing authority, actionable feedback-history/resolution-state changes, discovery/feedback/receipt policy, or required-check-policy drift uses trusted `refresh-review-input`, creates a new epoch, and invalidates current evidence according to explicit rules. Lifecycle-only draft/ready state is excluded from the content projection and receipt-bound separately.
- Missing connectors, unavailable linked authority, ambiguous base, shallow history, uncommitted review input, or non-remote head blocks.

### Plan 3 - Impact and coverage

- Independent semantic dependency mapping and contract/data-flow/user-journey mapping produce a union of changed and affected surfaces; a separate challenger attacks omissions.
- Every affected surface receives universal and repo/domain obligations. Maps/inventory carry a closed consequence vocabulary, and each obligation records its aperture and the deterministic maximum of scope, risk, and typed consequence floors. A sealed policy derives first-class epoch-namespaced complementary hypothesis records whose IDs are dispatchable and seal-bound. High-risk work gets opposite-polarity hypotheses and two distinct-contract strong reviews; `not-applicable` requires concrete counter-evidence plus receipt-verified review at the same floor, and high risk additionally requires a distinct-contract final-strong exemption challenger. Zero lens matches or unavailable independence blocks.
- Reviewer packages contain the complete patch, authorities, dependency context, generated-source provenance, and read-only repo access. Diff slices are navigation only and cannot hide context.

### Plan 4 - Reviewer execution

- Wire trusted harness adapters for deterministic/targeted command execution and reviewer dispatch. Command receipts bind exact command, working directory, snapshot head/tree, exit status, and output digest; copied success JSON cannot satisfy a check.
- Preserve intentionally different `reviewer-fast`, `reviewer-fixes`, `reviewer`, and `reviewer-strong` roles, but make their policy tiers explicit: hunk/file mechanical review may use `fast`; bounded fixes and surfaces use at least `focused`; cross-file, security, architectural, and high-risk review uses at least `strong`; whole-PR synthesis uses `final-strong`.
- Resolve each semantic role through `selecting-a-subagent` and a trusted harness authority outside the reviewed head. If an effective custom profile such as `reviewer-strong` satisfies that exact role, dispatch it by name without model/reasoning overrides and trust its baked route. A strength-qualified synthesis profile that consumes prior logs cannot satisfy blind final. Record readable profile bytes or an opaque adapter identity, plus a profile-resolution receipt.
- When no named custom profile exists, use the current runtime role mapping; only then fall back to qualified inheritance or an explicit route. Record the qualification source and reject absent profiles, mismatched role identity, profile overrides, or fallbacks below the assignment floor.
- Reviewer profiles are role-bounded and portable, with explicit assignment IDs, model/context/profile identity, structured JSON output, inspected surfaces, tested hypotheses, commands, findings, and uncertainties. Canonical Devin, Codex, and shared policy surfaces encode the same ladder without assuming provider names, prices, entitlements, or that every parent is the strongest available route.
- Role-discriminated outputs carry machine outcomes and canonical structured-artifact digests. Mapper/challenger records are derived from exact receipt-bound map/inventory/obligation bytes; adjudication and fix/exemption results cannot be interpreted from prose.
- Supply role-specific `blind-final` and `closure-auditor` contracts (either dedicated profiles or explicit trusted modes). The current vendored `reviewer-strong` contract consumes prior lens logs and is therefore ineligible for blind final until replaced or adapted; a harness-owned custom `reviewer-strong` may be trusted when its independently resolved contract already meets the role.
- Dispatcher validation independently re-fetches profile-resolution, launch, and completion receipts through the sealed per-kind issuer registry and compares kind/review/dispatch/epoch/fingerprint/subject/challenge scope. Launches consume post-registration single-use challenges and prove the realized profile/role/model/reasoning/context, instruction/data channels, exact injected bytes, and immutable route token. Completion binds exact raw report bytes and the ordered typed tool transcript. Duplicate keys, mapping races, caller-fabricated provenance, cross-review replay, wrong scope/issuer/namespace/profile/role/route, omitted assignments, structured-output substitution, malformed reports, unsupported outcomes, silent downgrade, and partial/tool-capped runs block.
- Blind-final context is a constructor-owned split between trusted instructions and inert raw snapshot/diff/authority data. Runtime reads use a snapshot-pinned role proxy that denies review state, prior reports/findings, and feedback/comments; every returned digest is transcript-bound. Free-form orchestrator context, hazard framing, copied conclusions, and instruction-channel injection are forbidden. Final and closure require affirmative `covered`, never `not-applicable`. Independent exemption review uses distinct role/profile-contract authority and canonical policy-derived hypotheses, not raw digest inequality.

### Plan 5 - Findings and fixes

- Every severity follows an immutable lifecycle. Only receipt/evidence-backed `fixed` or `false-positive` satisfies green; `fixing` remains unresolved, `accepted-risk` produces `reviewed-with-exceptions`, and all other dispositions block.
- Every finding-producing gate interrupts immediately. Explicit receipt-verified adjudication emits a strict per-finding `confirmed`, `false-positive`, or `contested` outcome that alone authorizes its compatible branch. False-positive, enter-fixing, targeted verification, fix review, fixed closure, and accepted-risk use separate payload/action contracts. A confirmed outcome permits exactly one of enter-fixing or accepted-risk; accepted-risk additionally requires durable human evidence and atomically terminates non-green. A published fix installs a new epoch and re-ascends; only already-recorded current targeted checks and fix/obligation re-review can close it as fixed.
- No cumulative historical count, stale regression link, overwritten origin field, missing round increment, circular resolution artifact, or arbitrary round cap can discard an unresolved issue or authorize green.

### Plan 6 - Independent closure and remote presentation

- Planning/archive/closeout mutations happen before the final freeze. A blind final reviewer and separate closure auditor both produce current receipt-verified attestations from fresh contexts through separately re-resolved role-specific final-strong contracts; a generic anchored `reviewer-strong`, disagreement, uncertainty, receipt/profile dispatch failure, unqualified fallback, or downgrade blocks.
- Required local checks are the exact complete set from a sealed external/base consumer policy; substituted harmless commands, wrong working directories, or omissions cannot count. Required hosted checks must succeed with policy item, app, workflow ID/path/definition ref/SHA, authorized event/trigger subject, policy inputs/configuration, check-run, workflow-run, run-attempt, and head identity; superseded attempts cannot count. Live provider fixtures cover multiple attempts and unauthorized manual triggers. Failures atomically materialize findings. The reserved `mark-ready-for-ci` action durably registers intent/idempotency, then challenge, then independently verified remote result; crash recovery and initially-ready no-op are explicit, and completion creates a CI candidate, not green.
- A candidate seal is content-bound. `reviewctl present` captures generation and exact state-byte digest, obtains a trusted fresh full remote observation, then re-locks and compares state immediately before evaluating and emitting a proof bound to both. A concurrent mutation retries or blocks. Remote evidence includes repository/PR/head, authority/feedback, full check-policy/workflow-definition/trigger/input identity, and exact current attempts; no stale generation or race can emit green.

### Plan 7 - Benchmark and cutover

- A versioned mutation/PR corpus spans every universal category. The reference set contains findings independently reproduced by two blinded frontier runs or confirmed by human adjudication.
- Every supported weaker-model/profile combination achieves 100% reference-finding recall on every one of at least three fresh trials per case, with zero false greens. New frontier-stable misses become permanent fixtures.
- Benchmark and dogfood PRs receive an additional blinded external frontier audit after internal green. The release target is zero frontier escapes across benchmark trials; production reports escape count per green PR by severity, obligation category, and tier that should have caught it, without presenting zero future escapes as a correctness guarantee.
- Adversarial pressure tests cover truncation, duplicate JSON keys, no matching lens, stale/wrong-trigger/wrong-definition CI, post-final and concurrent-presentation drift, provider-resolved feedback, anchoring through runtime tool reads or instruction injection, premature-green pressure, generated-source omissions, cross-lens fixes, unavailable tools/context/receipts, cross-review replay, fabricated receipts, repo-local policy/profile self-qualification, unsafe evidence paths, substituted local commands, unsupported `not-applicable`, missing canonical hypotheses/consequences, role-mismatched baked/opaque profiles, forbidden overrides, and profile/mapping drift.
- `SKILL.md` becomes a sub-500-word control plane; metrics, encoding normalization, ledger rendering, and summaries are derived CLI views rather than semantic graph nodes; version-1 authorities and parallel routes are removed; generated marketplace surfaces and consumer migration guidance are verified.

## Epic done gate

The epic is done only when all seven plans are merged, canonical staged CI and exact-SHA hosted CI pass, the Plan 7 benchmark has 100% per-trial reference recall, zero false greens, and zero frontier escapes, the marketplace/generated copies match canonical source, a fresh end-to-end dogfood review produces a presentation proof, and no user-facing path can label version-1 or incomplete version-2 work reviewed green.
