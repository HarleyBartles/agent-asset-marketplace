## Epic invariants

- No plan may weaken the green predicate in the v2 source spec.
- Each plan uses test-driven development and lands as a reviewable draft pull request before the next plan is written.
- Version-1 state, reports, and metrics may inform migration diagnostics but may not satisfy a version-2 green gate.
- A known `accepted-risk` finding may produce `reviewed-with-exceptions` but never green.
- A stored seal is only a candidate proof; human-facing green requires a fresh remote head, authority, and hosted-check revalidation plus witness-chain re-verification.
- Review dispatches follow a monotonic scope-to-reasoning ladder: broader aperture or greater consequence raises the minimum capability and reasoning floor; a lower tier may discover findings but may never satisfy a higher-tier obligation.
- A sealed review-assignment policy supplies non-null role floors and independence rules before obligation floors exist: mappers are at least strong/fresh/distinct, the scope challenger is final-strong/fresh/distinct, and adjudication/repair verification inherit source and linked-assignment floors and cannot validate their own source work.
- The blind final reviewer and closure auditor run in fresh independent contexts through role-specific profiles whose `allowed-tools` carry read/search only. Blindness is declared by contract and audited against the dispatch's transcript witness; contamination invalidates the review and opens a `review-process` finding.
- Witnessed evidence invariant: every load-bearing action - authority discovery, reviewer launch/completion, command execution, remote transition/observation, human decision - must leave a witness record bound to the hash-chained hook transcript or to a re-fetched GitHub record. Caller-authored success JSON, missing transcript entries, broken chains, reused `agent_id`/`tool_use_id`, or absent remote records block. The witness log is tamper-evident, not tamper-proof; the seal anchors its chain head into the pushed commit.
- The v2 workflow binds to Devin Desktop surfaces (hooks, `allowed-tools`, permission denies, subagent profiles, `gh`, `ask_user_question`) and is inert on every other harness. `reviewctl doctor` must pass before a review may start; a failed capability floor stops the skill instead of degrading.
- Every mutation holds an exclusive state lock across load, ingestion, validation, history, and compare-and-swap replacement. Immutable content identity is separate from epoch-bound evidence identity, so concurrent findings cannot be lost and unchanged bytes can remain historically bound across replacement epochs.
- Cutover requires both deterministic state-soundness tests and 100% per-trial recall of the versioned frontier-reference issue set for every supported weaker-model/profile combination.
- A valid in-scope issue first found by the cloud frontier reviewer after internal green is a calibration miss: it is classified by taxonomy, recorded in the `calibration` record, and becomes a permanent regression fixture. Green presents its miss rate honestly rather than implying unmeasured confidence.
- Source edits remain under `codex-marketplace/plugins/superpowers-plus/skills/iterative-review/` and `codex-marketplace/plugins/superpowers-plus/skills/selecting-a-subagent/`; `.agents/skills/` remains generated.
- Consumer repositories supply local commands and domain obligations. Portable source must not assume this repository's paths outside explicit repo-local adapters.
- Until Plan 7 cuts over and passes the benchmark plus pressure campaign, the existing skill must be described as review assistance, not proof of reviewed green.
- Until that cutover, the legacy skill is non-implicit and human-opt-in for each PR. Harness-designated frontier orchestrators, including `gpt-5.6-sol`, bypass it in favor of ordinary whole-change self-review plus canonical validation; its terminal `ready` value cannot authorize a green claim or draft-to-ready transition.
- The `reviewctl doctor` capability floor must pass before kernel execution; missing witness surfaces block implementation instead of being abstracted away.
- All JSON ingress rejects duplicate keys before canonicalization; witness verification binds full review/dispatch/epoch/fingerprint scope, not only a subject digest.
- Every record has an exhaustive constructor-owned projection, derived/external ID rule, and independent canonical-byte/hash golden vector; constructor/validator self-consistency is not acceptance proof.

## Plan acceptance boundaries

### Plan 1 - Evidence kernel

- `reviewctl doctor` proves the live Devin capability floor: the hooks pack emits Pre/PostToolUse records for orchestrator and subagent calls with `tool_use_id`/`agent_id`/`session_id`/`prompt_id`; a smoke dispatch proves `allowed-tools` strips unlisted tools and a denied-path probe is refused; `gh` auth and remote endpoints are reachable; required profiles carry `model:` pins; the witness store creates with private permissions. Any miss makes the skill inert for that session - it reports and stops.
- Strict version-2 state, exhaustive non-circular subject/witness projections with independent golden vectors, impact/challenged-coverage and canonical hypothesis records, typed consequence and role floors, structured exemption and review-repair proof, sealed local-check/review-assignment/witness/evidence-ingestion/hypothesis policies, duplicate-key rejection, review-owned immutable content plus epoch evidence bindings, cross-reference validation, chained history, locked compare-and-swap writes, discriminated feedback/review/check finding lifecycles, reserved ready-to-CI stage, monotonic re-ascent, and transient green evaluation exist behind one CLI. Plan 1 validates synthetic witness records only; live dispatch/check/remote actions stay blocked until Plans 2-6 wire the hook-transcript and `gh` witnesses.
- The shipped hooks pack (hook scripts + `.devin/hooks.v1.json` template + policy gating script) and the hash-chained witness log format are defined here; the log lives under the canonical off-repo review scratch store, is disposable after seal, and its chain head is bound into the pushed commit.
- Every known version-1 false-green/dead-route defect is a fixture proving legacy state cannot satisfy version 2; no version-1 routing repair remains because the baseline lens-triage correction already landed with the plan's publication commit.
- Missing one green predicate, incomplete authority manifest, unverified/fabricated/absent witness record, skipped or downgraded tier, wrong-role/overridden final profile, unqualified fallback, unsafe evidence source, unsupported exemption, stale bytes, persisted green, accepted risk, contaminated transcript audit, and malformed remote observation all fail closed.

### Plan 2 - Snapshot and authority

- A sealed versioned discovery policy rooted in an external or base authority - not the reviewed head - defines repository/directory-law and PR roots, typed authority edges, recursive fixed-point traversal, canonical deduplication, and ambiguity behavior. The discovery session is transcript-witnessed: the `authority-discovery` witness binds the enumeration session's transcript digest and the canonical manifest payload, and every fetched authority artifact is a content-bound evidence object; omission and self-qualification fixtures cover every root/edge.
- A sealed provider-history policy enumerates all actionable threads/change requests, including items resolved before initial freeze. Each creates a feedback authority and immutable provider/thread-keyed finding unless already closed by this review lifecycle. Provider Resolve changes the unresolved subset but cannot erase the finding; fixed/false-positive proof remains required. Live omission fixtures include resolve-before-freeze and resolve-between-refresh cases.
- Base/head/tree, content-bearing PR scope/metadata, governing authority, actionable feedback-history/resolution-state changes, discovery/feedback/witness policy, or required-check-policy drift uses `refresh-review-input`, creates a new epoch, and invalidates current evidence according to explicit rules. Lifecycle-only draft/ready state is excluded from the content projection and observed remotely instead.
- Missing connectors, unavailable linked authority, ambiguous base, shallow history, uncommitted review input, or non-remote head blocks.

### Plan 3 - Impact and coverage

- Independent semantic dependency mapping and contract/data-flow/user-journey mapping produce a union of changed and affected surfaces; a separate challenger attacks omissions.
- The two mappers run at least strong/high in fresh execution-distinct role contracts; the challenger runs final-strong/final-strong in a fresh execution and role contract distinct from both. Missing qualified independence blocks before obligations can be defined from a weak universe.
- Every affected surface receives universal and repo/domain obligations. Maps/inventory carry a closed consequence vocabulary, and each obligation records its aperture and the deterministic maximum of scope, risk, and typed consequence floors. A sealed policy derives first-class epoch-namespaced complementary hypothesis records whose IDs are dispatchable and seal-bound. High-risk work gets opposite-polarity hypotheses and two distinct-contract strong reviews; `not-applicable` requires concrete counter-evidence plus witnessed review at the same floor, and high risk additionally requires a distinct-contract final-strong exemption challenger. Zero lens matches or unavailable independence blocks.
- Reviewer packages contain the complete patch, authorities, dependency context, generated-source provenance, and read-only repo access. Diff slices are navigation only and cannot hide context.

### Plan 4 - Reviewer execution

- Wire reviewer dispatch and local command execution to the witness model. `run_subagent` calls are recorded by the hook transcript: the `dispatch-launch` witness binds exact task bytes, profile, and `tool_use_id`; the `dispatch-completion` witness binds the verbatim returned output and harness-assigned `agent_id`; the `tool-transcript` witness digests every call the dispatch made. No transcript entries, missing launch/completion pair, reused agent identity, or task bytes that smuggle prior findings all block.
- Local checks are engine-witnessed: `reviewctl` materializes the exact reviewed tree into a review-owned clean copy, records argv/cwd/environment digest, pre/post source digests, exit, and output digest; the `command-execution` witness binds them. There is no sandbox claim on Windows - commands needing credential or network isolation are hosted-CI obligations, not local ones. Dirty-checkout substitution, surviving children, and copied success JSON cannot satisfy a check because the recorded execution must hash-match the real run.
- Preserve intentionally different `reviewer-fast`, `reviewer-fixes`, `reviewer`, and `reviewer-strong` roles, but make their policy tiers explicit: hunk/file mechanical review may use `fast`; bounded fixes and surfaces use at least `focused`; cross-file, security, architectural, and high-risk review uses at least `strong`; whole-PR synthesis uses `final-strong`.
- Enforce the sealed review-assignment role table before route selection. Mapper/challenger/adjudicator/repair-verifier actions use fresh witness-distinct dispatches; adjudicators inherit the maximum source/linked-obligation floor and cannot reuse the source review's dispatch/role contract.
- Resolve each semantic role through `selecting-a-subagent` profile selection. A role-qualified profile with a `model:` pin and role-correct `allowed-tools` is dispatched by name without overrides and its baked route is trusted as declared. A strength-qualified synthesis profile that consumes prior logs cannot satisfy blind final. Record the resolved profile bytes hash and declared model; the completion record's self-reported model is captured honestly as a weak corroborating signal.
- When no qualifying profile exists, the dispatch blocks - there is no silent fallback to an unqualified profile or a looser route.
- Reviewer profiles are role-bounded, with explicit assignment IDs, structured JSON output, inspected surfaces, tested hypotheses, commands, findings, and uncertainties. Devin is the only supported harness; profile assets ship with correct `allowed-tools` and role contracts.
- Role-discriminated outputs carry machine outcomes and canonical structured-artifact digests. Mapper/challenger records are derived from the witnessed map/inventory/obligation bytes; adjudication and fix/exemption results cannot be interpreted from prose.
- Blind-final and closure-auditor roles ship dedicated profiles with read/search-only `allowed-tools`. Their declared-blindness is audited against the dispatch's `tool-transcript` witness; a contaminated audit invalidates the review and opens a `review-process` finding. Final and closure require affirmative `covered`, never `not-applicable`.

### Plan 5 - Findings and fixes

- Every severity follows an immutable lifecycle. Only witness/evidence-backed `fixed`, `review-repaired`, or `false-positive` satisfies green; `fixing` and `review-repairing` remain unresolved, `accepted-risk` produces `reviewed-with-exceptions`, and all other dispositions block.
- Every finding-producing gate interrupts immediately. Explicit witnessed adjudication emits a strict per-finding outcome plus `candidate-change` or `review-process` remediation class. Candidate change permits published `enter-fixing` or accepted risk. Review process permits same-snapshot `enter-review-repair` or accepted risk; the engine invalidates the typed target/downstream cut, reruns the earliest gate, requires independent current repair verification, closes only from existing proof, then reruns blind final and closure. No-op commits, byte-identical refresh, self-verification, and cross-branch reuse are rejected.
- No cumulative historical count, stale regression link, overwritten origin field, missing round increment, circular resolution artifact, or arbitrary round cap can discard an unresolved issue or authorize green.

### Plan 6 - Independent closure and remote presentation

- Planning/archive/closeout mutations happen before the final freeze. A blind final reviewer and separate closure auditor both produce current witnessed attestations from fresh contexts through separately resolved role-specific final-strong profiles; a generic anchored `reviewer-strong`, disagreement, uncertainty, transcript/profile dispatch failure, unqualified fallback, or downgrade blocks.
- Required local checks are the exact complete set from a sealed external/base consumer policy; substituted harmless commands, wrong working directories, or omissions cannot count. Required hosted checks must succeed with policy item, app, workflow ID/path/definition ref/SHA, authorized event/trigger subject, policy inputs/configuration, check-run, workflow-run, run-attempt, and head identity; superseded attempts cannot count. Live provider fixtures cover multiple attempts and unauthorized manual triggers. Failures atomically materialize findings. The reserved `mark-ready-for-ci` action runs the three-phase remote-transition: witnessed `gh pr ready` call, reconciling remote observation, committed outcome - with crash recovery and initially-ready no-op explicit. Completion creates a CI candidate, not green.
- A candidate seal is content-bound. `reviewctl present` captures generation and exact state-byte digest, obtains a fresh full remote observation via `gh` (the `remote-observation` witness records call bytes and full response digest), then re-locks and compares state immediately before evaluating and emitting a proof bound to both. A concurrent mutation retries or blocks. Remote evidence includes repository/PR/head, authority/feedback, full check-policy/workflow-definition/trigger/input identity, and exact current attempts; no stale generation or race can emit green. The seal binds the witness-chain head so presentation anchors the audit trail into the pushed commit.

### Plan 7 - Benchmark and cutover

- A versioned mutation/PR corpus spans every universal category. The reference set contains findings independently reproduced by two blinded frontier runs or confirmed by human adjudication.
- Every supported weaker-model/profile combination achieves 100% reference-finding recall on every one of at least three fresh trials per case, with zero false greens. New frontier-stable misses become permanent fixtures.
- Benchmark and dogfood PRs receive a cloud frontier review after internal green when usage permits; each run is a calibration sample. The release target is zero calibration misses across benchmark trials; production reports miss count per green PR by severity, obligation category, and tier that should have caught it, without presenting zero future misses as a correctness guarantee.
- Adversarial pressure tests cover truncation, duplicate JSON keys, no matching lens, stale/wrong-trigger/wrong-definition CI, post-final and concurrent-presentation drift, provider-resolved feedback, anchoring through runtime tool reads or instruction injection, premature-green pressure, generated-source omissions, cross-lens fixes, missing witness records, transcript-chain tampering, replayed `agent_id`/`tool_use_id`, fabricated transcript entries, repo-local policy/profile self-qualification, unsafe evidence paths, dirty/substituted local commands and surviving children, below-floor mapper/challenger/self-adjudication, same-snapshot process-repair deadlocks, canonical-projection field omission, unsupported `not-applicable`, missing canonical hypotheses/consequences, role-mismatched profiles, forbidden overrides, and profile/mapping drift.
- `SKILL.md` becomes a sub-500-word control plane; metrics, encoding normalization, ledger rendering, and summaries are derived CLI views rather than semantic graph nodes; version-1 authorities and parallel routes are removed; generated marketplace surfaces and consumer migration guidance are verified.

## Epic done gate

The epic is done only when all seven plans are merged, canonical staged CI and exact-SHA hosted CI pass, `reviewctl doctor` passes on a clean Devin install and the skill is confirmed inert on a non-Devin harness, the Plan 7 benchmark has 100% per-trial reference recall, zero false greens, and zero calibration misses across its trials, the marketplace/generated copies match canonical source, a fresh end-to-end dogfood review produces a presentation proof with a seal-anchored witness chain, and no user-facing path can label version-1 or incomplete version-2 work reviewed green.
