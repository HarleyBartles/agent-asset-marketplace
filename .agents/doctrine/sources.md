
## Scope

`sources/` (retired)

This scope is retired. The `sources/first_party/` and `sources/third_party/` custody trees were removed as part of the projection-layer retirement.

Canonical first-party skill custody now lives under `codex-marketplace/plugins/<plugin>/skills/`. Third-party upstream provenance is recorded under `provenance/`. Adaptation overlays live under `adapters/codex/`.

Defer to the repository root `AGENTS.md` for global repo doctrine.
