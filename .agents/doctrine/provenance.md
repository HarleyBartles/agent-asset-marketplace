# Provenance and License Guidance

This guidance covers upstream attribution and license obligations for first-party maintained marketplace source. There is no separate third-party skill source pool. A skill adapted from another open-source project remains canonical first-party source here, with its upstream basis and adaptation recorded honestly.

For source-backed or adapted skills, record the upstream project, URL or pinned revision where available, license, material adaptations, and required notices. Keep the evidence with canonical skill source or its authority references. Ensure every built plugin that contains the material carries the notices required by its license.

Do not call adapted work verbatim upstream content. Do not remove attribution or license terms when copying shared resources or including one skill in multiple products. See [custody and marketplace doctrine](custody-and-marketplace-doctrine.md) for source and build ownership.
