# Writing workflow

Use these stages in order. Read only the referenced specialist material needed for the artifact and observed problem.

1. Establish audience, purpose, facts, and hard constraints.
1. Draft or revise through $writing-with-clarity.
1. Apply a declared voice card through $writing-style when one is available.
1. Run $writing-style fatigue review only when evidence supports a material finding.
1. Re-run $writing-with-clarity as the final gate.

At the final gate, restore a removed qualification, fact, accessible formulation, or authorised voice choice if the style repair damaged it. A fatigue review may abstain; when it does, return the clear draft without a cosmetic rewrite. Never use this route to evade a detector or determine who wrote a text.
