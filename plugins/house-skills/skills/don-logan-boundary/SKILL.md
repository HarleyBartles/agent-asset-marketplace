# Don Logan Boundary

Use this skill to decide whether a live worker needs a strong governed correction to stop circling and complete an already-authorized task.

## Owned decision

Decide whether the generic Don Logan boundary triggers. This skill does not repair bad dispatches, supply missing authority, override blockers, or replace project-specific dispatch skills. Project wrappers supply local actor, repo, route, and protected-surface law.

## Hard boundaries

Don Logan is not stronger wording. It is a control-plane boundary for execution drift after authority is settled. Use it only when the task, route, constraints, protected surfaces, and success condition are already known and the worker is live or has just returned on the same task.

Do not use it for ordinary urgency, frustration, ambiguity, missing authority, real blockers, post-hoc verification doubt, or under-specified GPT dispatches.

## Progressive references

Read `references/don-logan-operating-contract.md` when deciding trigger status, checking preconditions and non-triggers, shaping a nudge or same-task follow-up, applying the pressure ladder, or explaining why Don Logan does not trigger.

## Minimal workflow

1. Confirm the task is authorized and the lawful actor/worker route is known.
2. Confirm scope, constraints, protected surfaces, and success condition are known.
3. Check whether the worker is live or just returned on the same task.
4. Decide whether the worker is substituting process, reports, rediscovery, justification, or refusal-shaped circling for execution.
5. If triggered, prepare only a bounded nudge or same-task follow-up and route it through the current dispatch gate.
6. If not triggered, use the safer route: wait, ordinary nudge, dispatch repair, clarification, or blocker.

## Output posture

Do not show a Don Logan receipt. If the boundary is triggered and passes the current dispatch gate, print the bounded nudge or follow-up only.
