# Domain truth boundaries

Use the `domain-truth-boundaries` guidance under `rooms-project-doctrine` when a claim or action crosses archive,
canon, manuscript, report, conversation, or machine-truth layers.

Core boundaries:

- Pit/archive preserves archive evidence, provenance, metadata, partial/missing state, reply chains, and ambiguity. The archive lane does not write canon.
- World/canon owns canon/world consistency from machine truth and reviewed source surfaces. The canon lane must not treat archive richness as character importance by itself.
- Manuscript/prose owns prose drafting from approved canon/partner truth packets. The manuscript lane does not resolve identity ambiguity independently.
- Machine truth is locked, append-only, no contradictions, no duplication, preserve ambiguity and provenance.
- Reports are reports, not truth.
- Conversation is not canon, archive evidence, manuscript authority, or publication proof.

Route to the lawful owner or required evidence surface rather than smoothing a boundary conflict into prose.
