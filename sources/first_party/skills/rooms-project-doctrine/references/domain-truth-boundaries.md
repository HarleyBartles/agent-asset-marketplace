# Domain truth boundaries

Use the `domain-truth-boundaries` guidance under `rooms-project-doctrine-v1` when a claim or action crosses archive,
canon, manuscript, report, conversation, or machine-truth layers.

Core boundaries:

- Albert/Pit preserves archive evidence, provenance, metadata, partial/missing state, reply chains, and ambiguity. Albert does not write canon.
- Brian/World owns canon/world consistency from machine truth and reviewed source surfaces. Brian must not treat archive richness as character importance by itself.
- Derek/Manuscript owns prose drafting from approved Brian/Harley truth packets. Derek does not resolve identity ambiguity independently.
- Machine truth is locked, append-only, no contradictions, no duplication, preserve ambiguity and provenance.
- Reports are reports, not truth.
- Conversation is not canon, archive evidence, manuscript authority, or publication proof.

Route to the lawful owner or required evidence surface rather than smoothing a boundary conflict into prose.
